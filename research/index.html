Research

I'm broadly interested in machine learning theory, in particular the design and analysis of statistically and computationally efficient algorithms. I'm especially interested in the simplest optimal algorithms, and learning in structured settings.

I work on statistical learning theory with Nikita Zhivotovskiy in Berkeley's Statistical Machine Learning group, and on deep learning theory with Michael DeWeese at BAIR.

I also work on computational nuclear physics at LLNL.
